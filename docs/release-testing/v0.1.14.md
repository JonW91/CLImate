## What changed in this release

- `-H` table now narrows its cells (18, 16, then 14 columns) to keep the multi-row art down to a 106-column terminal. Before, anything under 134 columns dropped to one-line icons.
- `-H` in a terminal under 106 columns shows a note and the list view instead of a wrapped table.
- `-H` with output piped or redirected always prints the full-width table.
- One-line icons (now only used with `--no-art`): sun icon no longer cut off, rain and snow showers no longer show `?`.
- Table labels can use the full cell width before being cut with `…`.
- The whole sun is now drawn in bold yellow, on clear sky and partly cloudy, with plain hyphens for the horizontal rays. Jon's terminal draws non-bold yellow hyphens as white. The partly cloudy side rays are hyphens instead of `~`; the colouring treats hyphens at the start and end of a line as rays and leaves the cloud outline grey.
- Lightning bolts are bold yellow, matching the sun.
- Cloud colour: bold dark grey (256-colour 238, chosen by Jon from a swatch on 2026-11-15) for rain, freezing rain, rain showers, thunderstorm and hailstorm; light grey for the rest. Drizzle and freezing drizzle moved from dark to light.
- New `climate --gallery` command: prints every weather icon at each art size, in colour, without fetching a forecast.
- `--hourly`: columns line up regardless of temperature length; "Freezing drizzle" is no longer cut to "Freezing dri...".
- Homebrew formula updated from 0.1.0-beta to 0.1.13 (tap commit 7fb1a30).
